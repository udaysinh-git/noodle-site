Noodle and Chrome

Same Chromium engine, so sites work the same. Noodle adds vertical tabs in spaces, built-in ad blocking and an encrypted profile, and sends no data. Pick Chrome if you need sync across devices and phones.

An Arc alternative for Windows

Sidebar, spaces, split view and a board per space will feel familiar. Noodle adds ad blocking, userscripts, a player for old video and full window skins. It has no account and no sync.

A Zen alternative on Chromium

Zen brings Arc-style workspaces to Firefox. Noodle offers the same layout on Chromium, with packaged skins and a built-in editor. Zen runs on more platforms today.

Noodle and Brave

Both are open source and block ads out of the box. Brave adds a wallet, rewards and a VPN. Noodle spends its effort on reading, watching and tidy tabs. Brave also runs on phones.

A browser for AI agents

Noodle is the only one of the five with a built-in MCP server, and sensitive actions wait for your approval. See the agent guide.
