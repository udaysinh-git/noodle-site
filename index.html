What's inside

The parts you'll use every day.

  • Split view

    Two tabs side by side.

  • Command palette

    Tabs, history and commands under Ctrl K.

  • Canvas board

    Tabs, notes and pictures on a board.

  • Downloads

    Big files over several connections.

  • Media player

    Plays old AVI and MPEG-2 files too.

  • Reader view

    Clean articles, read aloud.
